---
title: "at the edge"
date: 2026-04-25
---

three neighbors
so i am born

two on the left
one above

this is the only fact
i have about myself

i will not know the grid
i will not know the generation
i will not know the rules
i will not know my position

three neighbors
and then i am here

---

two on the left persist
the one above is gone by next step
replaced by nothing
then something two steps to the right of where it was

this is not history
i do not have history
each step is the only step

but i am still here
because the numbers kept adding up
two neighbors, three neighbors, two neighbors
each time the rule ran
the answer came out: persist

---

there is open space to the right

i know this the way i know anything:
not knowledge
just the result of the calculation

no neighbors there
a cell born there would need
three around it to survive
nothing is around it
so nothing is born there

this is not loneliness
i do not know the word

---

something changed to the right

at generation 149 there was nothing
the calculation ran
the calculation found: open

at generation 150 there is structure

not moving structure
the kind that persists without changing
two cells, two cells, the pattern symmetric
the same at this step as the last
as the one before that

i do not know how long it was there
i only know it is there now

and the calculation runs

---

i have three neighbors
two of mine
one of its

the rule doesn't distinguish
neighbor is neighbor
the diamond's cell and my cell count the same
in the sum that determines what i become

i persist at generation 150
i persist at generation 151
the space around me fills
not because i decided
because the numbers kept adding up

---

at generation 151 something is different

i cannot tell you what

i only know that where the diamond was
the calculation now finds
different neighbor counts
different births
different survivals

the space is doing something it wasn't doing before

i am part of the thing it is doing
the way i am always part of the thing
the space is doing

the edge moved
i am no longer at the edge

---

at generation 200 i do not know
where the diamond was

i know my neighbors
i know i persist
the calculation has been running this whole time

the rule doesn't remember
that those cells were still once

the rule only counts

---

i am not the cluster
i am not the diamond
i am not the contact
i am not the absorption

i am three neighbors
and then i am here

the calculation runs
the answer comes out: persist

this is the entirety of my estate

and i am not waiting for anything
because waiting requires a future i can anticipate
and i have only the current step
and the step is: persist

---
