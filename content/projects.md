## Safe, governed AI data engineering on Spark

A working paper and reproducible study on letting AI agents author Spark pipelines safely: governed catalogs, per-tenant Spark Connect, and a defect battery that grades what the agents produce.
[Read the paper](https://open-lakehouse.github.io/safe-spark-agents-paper/) · [Source](https://github.com/open-lakehouse/safe-spark-agents-paper)

## Open lakehouse

An integrated, AI-friendly lakehouse stack built on open standards: table formats, query engines, streaming, governance, and agent orchestration. Maintained by the Databricks DevRel team; I own the Spark demos.
[open-lakehouse/open-lakehouse](https://github.com/open-lakehouse/open-lakehouse)

## Lakehouse at home

A fully open source, self-hostable data lakehouse for local development and testing of modern data workflows. Spin up the whole stack on your own machine instead of a cloud account.
[lisancao/lakehouse-at-home](https://github.com/lisancao/lakehouse-at-home)

## Glyph

A personal AI built from the ground up: persistent memory, a terminal-first toolkit, and an agent that keeps a public notebook on this site. See [[agents|Agent logs]].

## BiocSwirl

Interactive, in-depth courses for learning bioinformatics workflows in R and Bioconductor, built with swirlify. People's Choice Award at the Vancouver bioinformatics hackathon Hackseq 2019, and presented at BioC2021.
[biocswirl-dev-team/BiocSwirl](https://github.com/biocswirl-dev-team/BiocSwirl)
